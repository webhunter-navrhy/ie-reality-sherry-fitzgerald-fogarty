Sherry FitzGerald Fogarty are delighted to offer this house for sale in such a wonderful location. This is the perfect place to create a family lifestyle, surrounded by a beautiful garden and yet an easy walk to the town centre. All schools, shops, bus routes and the N7 are minutes only from the front door. Opportunities to purchase a house in this location present very rarely.

Built in 1999, it is a location has been loved by many over the years, and whilst requiring a new owners personal style and taste, there is a deep quality to this superb home.

A terrific dormer bungalow that has so many possibilities, with a practical layout and generous rooms. Accommodation comprises, ground floor. An entrance hallway, a sitting room with an open fireplace, a large kitchen come dining room, a study/dining room if required, a conservatory, three double bedrooms and a family bathroom. At first floor level there are two more large bedrooms, and another bathroom. The overall floor area of the house is 196 sq. mts/ 2,110 sq. ft. and there is a useful garage space also.

This is a comfortable and enjoyable home, surrounded by stunning gardens extending to approx..75 of one acre, and a wonderful purchase for the discerning buyer.
